# Integration Blueprint: LaminarChain Features into eqats\n\n## 1. Data Engines\n- **Oracle Server**: Consume LaminarChain's price oracle for real-time FX and synthetic asset prices, feeding eqats's market data engine.\n- **Synthetic Asset Liquidity Pool**: Treat the pool's reserve balances and fToken supply as on-chain market depth data for synthetic EUR/JPY.\n- **Margin Trading Liquidity Pool**: Use pool utilization and financing fee rates as signals for funding-cost analysis.\n- **Money Market iToken & Interest Share**: Track iToken balances and interest allocation to compute yield curves for collateral assets.\n- **fToken Exchange Rate & iToken Accrual**: Derive synthetic-asset price and accrued yield for valuation models.\n\n## 2. Signal & Execution Logic\n- **fToken Mint/Burn**: Deposit collateral to mint fTokens (e.g., fEUR) and withdraw by burning, enabling eqats to gain synthetic exposure without leaving the chain.\n- **Liquidation Process**: Monitor liquidation events; trigger eqats to bid on discounted collateral or to hedge exposure.\n- **Financing Fee - Swap**: Read the swap-based financing fee from margin protocol to adjust carry-cost signals for leveraged positions.\n- **Margin Call / Stop Out**: Use margin level calculations to generate risk-adjusted entry/exit signals; automatically reduce leverage when margin level falls.\n- **Instant Liquidity Trading**: Execute market-style orders directly against the liquidity pools, leveraging infinite liquidity for synthetic and margin trades.\n- **Tokenized Positions**: Treat fTokens and margin-token representations as tradable instruments in eqats's portfolio, allowing cross-asset arbitrage and strategy stacking.\n\n## 3. Risk Engineering\n- **Collateral Ratio & Liquidation Threshold**: Enforce the same over-collateralization rules in eqats's risk engine; reject positions that would breach the threshold.\n- **Liquidation Incentive**: Allocate a portion of liquidation rewards to eqats's liquidity-provider module to incentivize risk-taking.\n- **Margin Metrics**: Compute equity, margin held, free margin, and margin level exactly as defined; use margin level < maintenance threshold as a stop-out trigger.\n- **Risk Caps**: Leverage the protocol's guarantee that trader liabilities are capped by locked margin; eqats can size positions based on this cap.\n- **Liquidity-Provider Risk Hints**: Feed pool utilization and insurance-fund data into eqats's LP risk monitor to assess exposure of providing liquidity to the pools.\n\n## 4. Implementation Steps\n1. Add a Substrate RPC client to eqats to query oracle, pool states, and token balances.\n2. Create adapters for fToken mint/burn, iToken interest accrual, and margin-position opening/closing.\n3. Integrate margin-level calculations into eqats's risk-checks pre-trade and post-trade.\n4. Build a liquidation-watcher that subscribes to on-chain liquidation events and feeds eqats's execution engine.\n5. Validate all synthetic-asset and margin trades against the protocol's collateral ratio before submission.\n6. Run unit tests using the Substrate node-template to ensure parity with LaminarChain's reference implementation.
